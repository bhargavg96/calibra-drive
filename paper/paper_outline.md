## Paper Sections

### 1. Introduction (1 page)
- Driving world models are increasingly used for planning
- **Problem**: Their predictions may be overconfident → unsafe planning
- **Our contribution**: CalibraDrive benchmark measuring calibration across models
- **Teaser figure**: Fig. 1

### 2. Related Work (0.5 page)
- Driving world models: OccWorld, Vista, Drive-WM, GAIA-1, UniSim
- Calibration in deep learning: Guo et al. 2017, temperature scaling
- Calibration for driving: C3 (arXiv:2512.05927), RELIOCC
- **Our gap**: Systematic cross-model calibration benchmark with driving-specific analysis

### 3. Method (1.5 pages)
- **3.1 Problem Formulation**: Define calibration for occupancy world models
- **3.2 Metrics**: ECE, MCE, Brier, AUROC (Tab. 1)
- **3.3 Spatial-Temporal Analysis**: Distance bins, prediction horizons
- **3.4 Recalibration**: Temperature scaling, conformal prediction, histogram binning
- **3.5 Downstream Planning**: MPC with uncertainty penalty

### 4. Experimental Setup (0.5 page)
- **Dataset**: nuScenes val split, 1500 scenarios (500 easy/medium/hard)
- **Models**: OccWorld (occupancy), Vista (video→occupancy), baselines
- **Protocol**: N=20 stochastic samples per scenario, 6-step horizon

### 5. Results (1.5 pages)
- **5.1 Overall Calibration** (H1): Tab. 1, Fig. 2 → models are overconfident
- **5.2 Spatial Analysis** (H3–H4): Tab. 2, Fig. 3a,d → worse at distance, worse for dynamic
- **5.3 Temporal Decay** (H2): Fig. 3b → ECE grows with horizon
- **5.4 Difficulty Stratification** (H8): Fig. 3c → hard scenarios are worst
- **5.5 Qualitative Analysis**: Fig. 4, Fig. 5, Fig. 6
- **5.6 Recalibration** (H5): Tab. 3, Tab. 4, Fig. 7 → temp scaling helps significantly
- **5.7 Downstream Planning** (H6–H7): Tab. 5, Fig. 8 → calibrated uncertainty improves safety

### 6. Discussion & Limitations (0.25 page)
- Synthetic-to-real gap in calibration findings
- Model-specific vs. architecture-level conclusions
- Computational cost of multi-sample inference

### 7. Conclusion (0.25 page)
- World models are poorly calibrated → safety implications
- Simple recalibration methods are effective
- Call to action: calibration should be a standard evaluation axis
